SUNDER COMPUTERS - ADD PRODUCT V3

This version fixes the Prisma schema mismatch and adds retail price comparison.

Prisma schema now includes Product fields, ProductImage and RetailListing.
RetailListing stores retailer name, price and URL. Average retail price is calculated automatically from all valid retail listings.

After copying the project, run: npx prisma db push
Then restart the dev server.

Do NOT use prisma migrate reset or delete dev.db.

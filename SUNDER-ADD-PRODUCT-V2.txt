SUNDER COMPUTERS - ADD PRODUCT V2

What changed:
- Product photos now upload to /public/uploads/products during local development.
- Uploaded image URLs are saved as ProductImage records.
- First photo is automatically the primary image.
- Photos can be reordered before saving.
- Live profit, margin and retail saving calculation.
- Stock number duplicate protection.
- Purchase date is converted to Prisma DateTime.
- Save Product creates the database record and image records.

Important:
- This local upload method is suitable for development. For production hosting, move product images to persistent object storage/CDN rather than relying on the local public folder.
- /admin/products is not authenticated yet. Authentication/admin permissions are a later step.
- Do not mix this Sunder project with the WP Motors project.
